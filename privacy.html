Odeeffannoo maal kuusna?

Appiin Video Ergaa odeeffannoo dhuunfaa kee hin walitti qabu, hin ergu, hin gurgurus. Akkaawuntii hin barbaachisu.

Suuraa, sagalee fi video

Suuraan ofii kee, sagaleen waraabdu, muuziqaan filattu fi video hojjette hundi bilbila kee keessatti qofa hafa. Server keenyatti hin ol-fe'amu. Video kee yoo qoodde, bakka itti qoodde (fakkeenyaaf WhatsApp) bulchiinsa isaanii jala ta'a.

Beeksisa

Gara fuulduraatti appiin kun beeksisa Google (AdMob ykn AdSense) agarsiisuu danda'a. Google beeksisa dhiyeessuuf eenyummaa meeshaa (device ID) ykn kuukii fayyadamuu danda'a. Seera Google: policies.google.com/privacy.

Maayikii fi suuraa

Maayikiin sagalee waraabuuf qofa, suuraan fuula fakkii irratti kaa'uuf qofa, eeyyama kee yeroo gaafamtu fayyadama.

Daa'imman

Daa'imni umrii 13 gadi maatii ykn eegduu wajjin fayyadamuu qaba.

Privacy · English

Last updated: October 2026

What data do we collect?

Video Ergaa does not collect, send or sell your personal data. No account is needed.

Photos, voice and videos

Your photo, recorded voice, chosen music and finished videos stay on your phone only. They are not uploaded to our servers. When you share a video, it is subject to the rules of the app you share it with (for example WhatsApp).

Ads

In the future the app may show Google ads (AdMob or AdSense). Google may use a device identifier or cookies to show ads. See Google's policy: policies.google.com/privacy

Microphone and photos

The microphone is used only to record your voice, and photos only to put your face on a character, each after you give permission.

Children

Children under 13 should use the app with a parent or guardian.
